# Psych-Therapies.com Website Redesign Audit & Strategy
**Prepared for: Psych-Therapies Clinical Psychology Practice**  
**Date: June 2026**

---

## Executive Summary

The current psych-therapies.com website has fundamental issues that prevent it from effectively attracting clients, building trust, and converting inquiries. The site feels outdated, lacks clear navigation, and fails to communicate the practice's expertise and value proposition. This document outlines critical redesign priorities that will improve user experience, establish credibility, and increase client inquiries.

**Key Finding:** The website reads like an information dump rather than a welcoming, trust-building entry point. Prospective clients arrive anxious and need reassurance—the current design delivers neither.

---

## CRITICAL ISSUES IDENTIFIED

### 1. **Weak Hero Section & Missing Value Proposition**
**Current State:**
- Opens with three profile photos and no headline
- No clear statement of what the practice does or why someone should choose them
- No emotional connection or trust-building message

**Impact on Clients:**
- Visitors don't immediately understand what they're looking at
- No reason to stay beyond 3 seconds
- Anxiety-driven visitors get no reassurance

**Fix Priority:** CRITICAL

---

### 2. **Confusing Navigation Structure**
**Current Issues:**
- "More Menu pages" dropdown is unclear
- Menu labels are vague ("Copy-of-about-us" visible in URLs—unprofessional)
- 8+ menu items with no clear hierarchy
- "Contact Us" buried in navigation instead of prominent CTA

**Impact on Clients:**
- Difficult to find information they need
- Feels disorganized and unprofessional
- High bounce rate from navigation confusion

**Fix Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. **Outdated Visual Design**
**Current Issues:**
- Flat, generic color palette with no modern visual appeal
- Typography is basic and uninviting
- No design hierarchy—everything feels equally important
- Images appear low-quality and stock-like
- No visual consistency across sections

**Impact on Clients:**
- First impression is "this practice is outdated"
- Undermines credibility despite team's qualifications
- Looks like a 2010s template, not 2026

**Fix Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. **Weak Trust Signals & Credibility**
**Current Issues:**
- Only one visible testimonial in slideshow
- Credentials not highlighted (all are Dr./qualified, but not emphasized)
- Partnership logos shown but no explanation
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof
- No "why choose us" differentiation

**Impact on Clients:**
- Hard to believe this practice is reputable
- No reason to trust over competitors
- Insurance partnerships shown but not explained

**Fix Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. **Contact Form Buried & Low Conversion**
**Current Issues:**
- Contact form at bottom of page, below fold
- Email contact (psychtherapies1@gmail.com) displayed prominently instead of form
- No clear CTA or urgency
- Form doesn't set expectations ("We'll respond within X hours")

**Impact on Clients:**
- Many leave without contacting
- Those who do use email may get slower response
- No conversion tracking/follow-up

**Fix Priority:** CRITICAL

---

### 6. **Unclear Client Journey & Vague Process**
**Current Issues:**
- Three-step process is too simplistic
- "Assessment (up to 60 minutes)" doesn't explain what happens
- No timeline expectations (1-2 weeks mentioned but buried)
- No clear description of what to expect in first session

**Impact on Clients:**
- Anxious clients don't know what to expect
- Creates friction and hesitation
- Increases abandonment rates

**Fix Priority:** HIGH

---

### 7. **Services Not Clearly Differentiated**
**Current Issues:**
- "Psychological Therapies," "Remote Therapy," "Supervision" all equal weight
- No explanation of who each service is for
- No pricing or session duration information
- Approaches/modalities mentioned but not explained (EMDR, CBT, etc.)

**Impact on Clients:**
- Unsure if practice offers what they need
- Can't make informed decision
- Forced to contact to ask basic questions

**Fix Priority:** HIGH

---

### 8. **Missing Critical Information**
**Current Issues:**
- No pricing or fee structure
- No crisis/emergency contact info
- No FAQ section
- No insurance details beyond logos
- No "Why us?" section
- No staff bios (only names)
- No therapist specialties clearly listed

**Impact on Clients:**
- Can't determine affordability before contacting
- No emergency plan if in crisis
- Unanswered questions lead to abandonment

**Fix Priority:** CRITICAL

---

### 9. **Poor Content Hierarchy & Mobile Experience**
**Current Issues:**
- Information feels scattered across page
- No scannable sections for mobile users
- Long paragraphs with no visual breaks
- Menu collapses but unclear how
- No responsive image sizing

**Impact on Clients:**
- Mobile users (60% of traffic) have poor experience
- Can't quickly find information
- High bounce rate on mobile

**Fix Priority:** HIGH

---

### 10. **Lack of Emotional Connection**
**Current Issues:**
- Copy is clinical and corporate ("We work in a friendly and warm manner")
- Shows don't tell—photos and testimonials don't build warmth
- No acknowledgment of client anxiety/concerns
- "So how does it all work?" feels transactional, not caring

**Impact on Clients:**
- Doesn't feel like a "safe space" (which is core message)
- Doesn't address the emotional component of seeking help
- Misses opportunity to convert anxious visitors

**Fix Priority:** MEDIUM

### **Phase 6: Visual Design System (Week 3-4)**

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Warm teal/sage green (#2D7A7A or #4A9B8E)
  - Psychology associations with calm, trust, growth
- Secondary: Soft warm white/cream (#F5F1E8)
- Accent: Gentle gold (#D4AF8F)
- Text: Dark slate (#2C3E50)

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Friendly, modern serif (Georgia, Garamond)
- Body: Clean sans-serif (Inter, Segoe UI)
- This creates "professional but warm" feeling

**Photography:**
- Show practice space (warm, light, welcoming)
- Team photos: professional but smiling/relaxed
- Diversity reflected in imagery
- NO stock imagery—real space, real people

**Layout Principles:**
- Maximum content width: 1200px
- Generous white space (not cramped)
- Cards for services (not walls of text)
- Icons to break up content
- Responsive design (mobile-first approach)

## WHY THIS MATTERS FOR CLIENT ACQUISITION

### Current Problem:
Your practice has excellent credentials and outcomes, but the website tells a different story. Anxious clients arriving at your site see:
- Outdated design = "Are they still in practice?"
- Unclear information = "I don't understand what I'm getting"
- Buried contact form = "They don't want to hear from me"
- No testimonials = "Is this actually working for people?"

**Result:** They leave and contact a competitor.

### After Redesign:
- Modern, professional design says "This is a current, thriving practice"
- Clear information says "I know exactly what to expect and it's right for me"
- Prominent CTAs say "You're welcome here, let's help you"
- Multiple testimonials say "Real people are getting better here"

**Result:** They book an assessment.

## ADDITIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS

### 1. **Collect More Testimonials**
- Request from existing clients
- Include photo, name, date, and specific benefit
- Target different client types (anxiety, trauma, couples)
- Aim for 8-10 testimonials minimum

### 2. **SEO Optimization**
- Target keywords: "therapist near me," "EMDR [location]," "anxiety therapy [location]"
- Add schema markup for healthcare provider
- Blog posts on therapy approaches, mental health topics
- Local SEO (Google Business Profile)

### 3. **Booking System Integration**
- Consider Calendly, Acuity Scheduling, or similar
- Allow direct online booking (reduces friction)
- Automated confirmation emails
- Reminder emails reduce no-shows

### 4. **Analytics & Tracking**
- Install Google Analytics 4
- Track form submissions, CTA clicks, testimonial views
- Monitor bounce rate by page
- A/B test CTAs and messaging

### 5. **Social Proof Enhancement**
- Google Reviews (ask satisfied clients to leave reviews)
- Psychology board registrations/certifications prominently displayed
- Insurance partnerships clearly explained
- Client success statistics (if available)

### 6. **Mobile-First Approach**
- Design for mobile first, then scale up
- Touch-friendly form inputs
- Vertical menu works well on mobile
- Fast loading times (compress images)

### 7. **Accessibility Standards**
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Proper heading hierarchy
- Alt text for all images
- Keyboard navigation support
- Color contrast ratios
